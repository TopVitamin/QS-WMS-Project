# PDA支持B2B拣货与扫描发货

PDA支持B2B业务，可以为仓库提供无纸化拣货，出库环节支持扫描出库，提升B2B出库的速度和准确率。PDA实现B2B业务拣货和扫描出库步骤如下：

**1.WMS角色设置部分开启PDA的B2B拣货和发货的权限**

![image.png](https://wms-1382655522-cos.jfwms.com/prod/temp/system/img/77/20260603121430/4db3015b367fef3f49726e70516d5747.png)

![image.png](https://wms-1382655522-cos.jfwms.com/prod/temp/system/img/77/20260603121524/7a5dab63d4b08d9b0c32cafbb5570fdc.png)

**2.权限开启后PDA页面会出现B2B拣货和出库的入口**

![image.png](https://wms-1382655522-cos.jfwms.com/prod/temp/system/img/77/20260601013047/7f1edaad4b352fa9d8bf3c13fa2cd7ca.png)

**3.扫描拣货，支持按产品和按箱进行拣货**

![image.png](https://wms-1382655522-cos.jfwms.com/prod/temp/system/img/77/20260601013138/32f2b2dea7e0b0366d49aa96417eccb2.png)

![image.png](https://wms-1382655522-cos.jfwms.com/prod/temp/system/img/77/20260601013335/0c58cdd5ab2dab6fb3f2a1d9709aec6b.png)

![image.png](https://wms-1382655522-cos.jfwms.com/prod/temp/system/img/77/20260601013400/0f20ce161f73559dbbe1a0b07f41d849.png)

**4.拣货完成后，订单再待出库状态的时候可以扫描出库单号或者运单号进行扫描发货**

![image.png](https://wms-1382655522-cos.jfwms.com/prod/temp/system/img/77/20260601013427/f21060c279ae23073061f99a51e4fbb5.png)
