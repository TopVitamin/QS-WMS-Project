# 如何注册、授权、使用dadan.com物流

**一、定义**

dadan.com物流是美国物流，需要授权使用的海外仓看这个帮助教程哈。

** 二、操作流程**

**第一步：获取dadan.com授权**

**登录dadan.com-账号信息-基础信息-api信息-复制apikey和apisecret**

![图片](https://download.jfwms.com/prod/static/back_help/img/74/20250918015531/90eb30902aadd4d6f7dd0a49063fea3a.jpg)

#### 
**第二步：在极风海外仓系统添加dadan物流商**

![image.png](https://wms-1382655522-cos.jfwms.com/prod/temp/system/img/143/20260525090520/008ca02a8614e19f1f281959eeb6df4a.png)

![image.png](https://wms-1382655522-cos.jfwms.com/prod/temp/system/img/143/20260525090618/d1488cf5a6a93de14d33b44643422866.png)

![image.png](https://wms-1382655522-cos.jfwms.com/prod/temp/system/img/143/20260525090737/94331a4986349f956b0c1a628c87b6b8.png)

**第三步：开启物流渠道（物流渠道名称和代码请与物流商确认，若不正确则会影响系统向物流商获取运单号）**

![image.png](https://wms-1382655522-cos.jfwms.com/prod/temp/system/img/143/20260525090810/423a5a56f50c50154bf663e75ce6a2d1.png)

![image.png](https://wms-1382655522-cos.jfwms.com/prod/temp/system/img/143/20260525091010/dbd813a58d357ee65a121baa7b8caf39.png)

**第四步：海外仓为客户开启物流渠道（开启后卖家可在OMS卖家端、ERP端看到这个渠道）**

![image.png](https://wms-1382655522-cos.jfwms.com/prod/temp/system/img/143/20260525091141/fa252827c4b4d677533b91576abd683c.png)
