# 极风WMS如何通过shipbest打单

#### 1、获取shipbest的ID和Token找物流商获取，物流商给你开户完成后，可以获取到你的ID和TOKEN；或者登录到shipbest的oms端获取，物流商开户完成后，你会收到一封开户邮件，注册激活。

![图片](https://download.jfwms.com/prod/static/back_help/img/21/20250911115056/50db5dd0ef91ed17914846fb83d0b0fa.jpg)

![图片](https://download.jfwms.com/prod/static/back_help/img/21/20250911115305/693354b9bdbb9796e7f7ba37aec4b3a6.jpg)

进入设置，API配置页面

![图片](https://download.jfwms.com/prod/static/back_help/img/21/20250911115210/7c0627d2515bbca9e125542bb9d13f9d.jpg)

#### 2、登录极风WMS，进入物流、物流商管理页面

![image.png](https://wms-1382655522-cos.jfwms.com/prod/temp/system/img/143/20260525091427/aeab05d14d5d00b194a9727fc8f2e065.png)

添加物流商、在“物流商”选择shipbest，将上面的ID和Token复制进来，点击授权即可

![image.png](https://wms-1382655522-cos.jfwms.com/prod/temp/system/img/143/20260525091510/5b2720a88e1eecfb2910a3d592420e84.png)

![image.png](https://wms-1382655522-cos.jfwms.com/prod/temp/system/img/143/20260525091602/893808dd2a20efd6d3f3cc9d959a59ab.png)

#### 3、点击授权完成后，点击“渠道管理”按钮，开启物流渠道

![image.png](https://wms-1382655522-cos.jfwms.com/prod/temp/system/img/143/20260525091641/34e9792a6f528cdcd086db6e6b25e757.png)

![image.png](https://wms-1382655522-cos.jfwms.com/prod/temp/system/img/143/20260525091707/d1488cf5a6a93de14d33b44643422866.png)

#### 4、打开“客户物流渠道”页面，点击“新增”按钮

![image.png](https://wms-1382655522-cos.jfwms.com/prod/temp/system/img/143/20260525092002/e544f47bea8941fa7f83efb8def0e009.png)
